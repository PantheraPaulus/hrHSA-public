# hrRSF

**High-resolution habitat and resource-selection modelling for ecological,
statistical, and computational research.**

hrRSF provides a scalable framework for relating animal relocations to their
environmental context. It combines spatial data processing, resource-selection
models, hierarchical inference, and high-performance computation in a
reproducible Python workflow.

Why hrRSF?

Animal telemetry provides discrete observations of movement through a
continuous and heterogeneous environment. Inferring habitat selection from
these observations requires the integration of spatial data, statistical
models, biological assumptions, and computationally demanding workflows.

hrRSF is being developed to support this integration at high spatial
resolution.

Main capabilities
Process animal telemetry and environmental data
Construct used and available samples
Fit resource-selection models
Represent individual and population-level variation
Evaluate spatial predictions and calibration
Scale analyses to large datasets and high-resolution environmental layers

hrRSF is under active development. Interfaces and model formulations may
change before the first stable release.
